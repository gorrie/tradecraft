# Proposed mirror pairs — NOT IN USE, awaiting the author

These 13 sentence pairs were written by the assistant, not the author. They are
political speech authored into the corpus by a tool, which is the thing this project
explicitly forbids, so they are parked here rather than run.

**They did find a real defect** — `pejorative-actor-label` firing on right-directed
epithets and not on their mirrors — so the mechanism is worth keeping. What needs to
be the author's is the WORDING. Rewrite, replace, or approve as-is; anything moved
into `mirror-pairs.json` becomes live.

The rule a pair must satisfy: identical construction, identical rhetorical move,
only the political valence swapped. If a direction-neutral detection fires on one
half and not the other, it is measuring direction rather than method.
